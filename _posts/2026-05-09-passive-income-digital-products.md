## Beyond the PDF
Selling digital products in 2026 isn't just about e-books. The market has moved toward **interactive assets**.

### What’s Selling in 2026?
1. **AI Workflows and Templates:** People don't want to learn the tool; they want the result. Sell your optimized "prompt chains" or automation workflows.
2. **Custom Jekyll Themes:** As developers move back to static sites for speed and security, the demand for premium, accessible Jekyll themes is skyrocketing.
3. **Niche Datasets:** Cleaned, labeled data for specific industries (like agriculture or local real estate) is liquid gold for small businesses training their own local AI models.

### Platforms to Watch
While Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy remain strong, decentralized marketplaces are gaining traction, offering lower fees for creators.
