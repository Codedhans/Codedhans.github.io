## The Privacy Revolution
Third-party cookies are dead and buried. In 2026, the focus has shifted to **Privacy-Preserving APIs** and Zero-Knowledge Proofs.

### What This Means for Developers
You need to build with privacy by design. We are seeing a massive shift toward server-side tracking and first-party data strategies. If your app isn't privacy-first, users (and regulators) will abandon you.
