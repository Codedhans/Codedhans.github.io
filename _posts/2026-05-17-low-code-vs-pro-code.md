## The Coexistence
The fear that low-code platforms would replace developers has vanished. Instead, we have reached a state of **hybrid development**.

### The Division of Labor
* **Low-Code:** Used for rapid internal tooling and simple CRUD apps.
* **Pro-Code:** Reserved for high-performance, custom-logic, and core infrastructure.

Smart developers in 2026 are using low-code to automate the "boring stuff" so they can focus on complex problem-solving.
