## Beyond the Zoom Call
Remote work in 2026 doesn't look like it did in 2020. We’ve moved into the world of **Spatial Presence**.

### VR and the Metaverse Office
With the release of lightweight, high-fidelity headsets, "remote" now feels like being in the same room. For freelancers, this means global teams are tighter than ever, and the "time-zone gap" is closing thanks to AI-driven asynchronous communication tools.
